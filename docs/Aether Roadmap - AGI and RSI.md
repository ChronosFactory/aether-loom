# Aether Roadmap: AGI and RSI

**Governing principle:** Aether must become more capable tomorrow because of what it learned, discovered, built and
validated today. Eventually it must become better at improving itself.

**The defining question for every phase:** what can Aether independently accomplish today that it could not
accomplish yesterday, and what evidence proves the improvement came from its own development?

## Standing rules

- **Not an LLM.** Aether is not a language model or a retrieval system. Its foundation is persistent knowledge,
  associative learning, compositional reasoning, executable concepts, memory, experimentation and developmental
  growth.
- **Tools are not Aether.** External tools may observe or execute. The learning, decisions and improvements credited
  to Aether must come from its own mechanisms. No undisclosed external coding model.
- **Credit precisely.** Every claim separates what Aether did from what its tools or we supplied.
- **Scientific integrity.** Frozen protocols, unseen tasks, independent confirmation, ablations, matched controls,
  explicit compute accounting, and preserved negative results.
- **Isolation and rollback.** Every modification starts in an isolated snapshot with resource limits, regression
  tests and rollback. The live Core is never silently changed to improve a benchmark. Promotion needs validated
  benefit, no unacceptable regressions, and the author's approval.
- **What counts as RSI.** RSI is shown only when Aether improves the mechanisms that produce its future improvements.
  More facts, more benchmarks or a human-written component do not count.
- **Phases advance on evidence.** A phase advances when its claim survives evaluation, not when its code is done.
  Keep a versioned capability registry: demonstrated / preliminary / aspiration.

## Phases (ordered by dependency, not by date)

| # | Phase | Exit criterion (short) | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Developmental measurement and integrity | A reproducible instrument across seeds separates learning gains, regressions and compute cost, with REAL vs SCRAMBLED timelines and fixed-budget anytime curves, and no Core change | Developmental Measurement System |
| 1 | Reliable knowledge representation and reasoning | Aether learns unseen relational facts, applies them to unfamiliar questions, distinguishes reversed relations, and shows causal dependence under ablation | Relational representation subsystem |
| 2 | Autonomous knowledge acquisition and scientific reasoning | Aether constructs a useful representational extension, validates it on unseen measurements, and reuses it on a separate problem without human selection of the winner | Autonomous Scientific Discovery Engine |
| 3 | Generalized skills and hierarchical abstraction | Multi-level abstractions built autonomously work on unfamiliar tasks beyond the direct-search limit, beating random-library, disabled-generation and no-transfer controls | Hierarchical Skill Learning and Consolidation |
| 4 | Autonomous software engineering | Aether diagnoses, implements, tests and repairs changes across unseen repositories, and prior programming experience improves later performance | Aether Autonomous Coding System |
| 5 | Self-diagnosis and autonomous curriculum | Across domains, its diagnoses and chosen interventions beat no-intervention and matched human picks under equal budgets, and it rejects low-value interventions | Self-Directed Learning and Diagnostic System |
| 6 | Autonomous cognitive component construction | Aether finds an architectural limit, builds a bounded remedy, validates the gain on unseen tasks without bad regressions, and deployment stays human-approved | Controlled Cognitive Self-Modification Framework |
| 7 | Recursive improvement of the improvement process | Over several controlled generations, at least one improvement is to the improvement process itself, and later generations improve more effectively than earlier ones | Experimental RSI System |
| 8 | Cross-domain general intelligence | Sustained, independently replicated cross-domain adaptation and transfer; AGI needs its own demanding standard | General Intelligence Evaluation Report |
| 9 | Integrated lifelong intelligence | A longitudinal evaluation shows acquired capabilities, transfer, retention, and improving ability to learn and improve | Persistent Developmental Intelligence |

## Immediate priority (2026-11-03)

1. **Formula-language construction (phase 2).** Record which decisions are Aether's and which are human-specified.
   Aether uses a bounded grammar to construct competing extensions, judges them on withheld observations, and reuses
   them on a different problem.
   - **Active:** T295.
2. **Coding curriculum (phase 4, in parallel).** Small unfamiliar repositories. Can Aether infer a change, implement
   it, read the test results and learn from them with its own mechanisms?
3. **Baselines kept:** T294 (Planck from COBE), T291 (self-diagnosis), hierarchical-composition evidence, and the
   developmental battery protocols.

**First integrated milestone:** Aether independently diagnoses a limitation, constructs a remedy, expresses or
implements it, validates it, and reuses it on a different problem.

**Final success criterion:** Aether demonstrably becomes more capable through experience, independently constructs
useful cognitive capabilities, and eventually improves the mechanisms that produce its improvements.
